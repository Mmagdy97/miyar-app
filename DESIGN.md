# Design: Clear Slate + Teal (v2)
Research basis: bottom tab bar (<=5), 44px targets, one hero metric per card, one accent, colour only for status, WCAG AA.
- Ground #F4F6F9, surface #FFFFFF, line #E3E7EE, ink #0F172A, muted #475569/#64748B.
- Accent teal #0F766E (hover #0B5F58); status colours unchanged (red/amber/green) and always paired with text/icon.
- Mobile: BottomNav (4 tabs + More drawer); `.stat-grid` = swipeable single KPI cards at 82% width; compact chips.
- Radius 6/8/12, flat depth. Dark mode generated by scripts/gen-dark.py.
