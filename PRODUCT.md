# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and staff of small and mid-size contracting and interior-design offices in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE (later Kuwait, Jordan, Turkey), who run projects over WhatsApp and Excel. Four roles: Admin (office owner/manager), Site Engineer (on site, often weak internet, mostly on phone), Technical Office (drawings, RFIs, quantities; must not see cost/margin), Client (follows progress, approves change orders and selections).

## Product Purpose
Miyar (معيار) is an Arabic-first RTL SaaS to run contracting and interior-design projects: smart BOQ with live margin, tasks and site reports, petty cash, certificates/retentions, procurement, change orders, FF&E selections, moodboard, client portal, e-invoice drafts. Success: an office creates a project, a BOQ and a client link in the first week, and keeps using it daily.

## Positioning
The light Arabic (also Turkish/English) alternative for small contractors and designers: what heavy ERPs do, at a lower price (unlimited users, priced by active projects), with locally fitted finance (petty cash, certificates, e-invoicing).

## Operating Context
Used daily on desktop in the office and on phones on site. RTL by default; ar/tr/en; seven currencies (EGP, SAR, AED, KWD, JOD, TRY, USD). Offline PWA. Currently demo data in localStorage; Supabase backend pending.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack: Vite 8, React 19, Tailwind 4, plain JS, lucide-react; i18n flat dictionaries (ar/en/tr parity); dark theme generated by scripts/gen-dark.py; print CSS for BOQ/quotes.
- Technical Office hides cost/margin in UI (server-side protection pending).
- E-invoicing is drafts only; client approvals/signature are local acceptances, not legally certified.
- Currency amounts are not converted from EGP base (open decision).

## Brand Commitments
Name "معيار / Miyar" (domain/trademark check pending). Existing brand colour is orange; the user chose to redesign the whole UI as a calm, precise work tool (confirmed in chat 2026-11-12). Redesign scope: landing page and all four role portals together.

## Evidence on Hand
No real customers, testimonials or metrics yet; do not fabricate. Demo data only. PRD: Magdy project docs ("Miyar PRD").

## Product Principles
1. Numbers first: margin, cash and certificate figures must be scannable at a glance.
2. Site-ready: every field workflow works one-handed on a phone with weak connectivity.
3. Role-safe: each role sees only what it should.
4. Arabic is the primary language, not a translation; RTL layout is designed, not mirrored.
5. Honest claims: drafts are labelled as drafts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic/Turkish/English, RTL; touch targets suited to site use; WCAG AA contrast expected in light and dark themes.
